  - all 24 labellings of Q2, which give 16 × 5 and 8 × 6. The count is 6 exactly when labels 1 and 2 sit on opposite vertices;
  - on Q3: lex = 16, Gray = 21, parity = 16;

- Random labellings for d = 1..9 (5/30/200/200/100/50/20/20/10 of them). Each is checked three ways:
  - main counter against the forward DP (paths ending at v);
  - main counter against explicit DFS, for d <= 7;
  - invariance under a random automorphism of Q_d.
- Gray orders for d = 1..9: main counter against the forward DP; also against DFS for d <= 4.
- Format, 34 CLI cases:
