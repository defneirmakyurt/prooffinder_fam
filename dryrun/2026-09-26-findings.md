## Phase 1L (H-C1) — the literature route beat the blind route
28. Literature H-C1-006 (397 s, 89k tokens) proved **both lower bounds by hand, with no search at all**: it re-proves the general bound U(G) >= |E(G)|+1 in full, then analyses its equality case, which forces `(d-1)m = 2^{d-1}(d-2)+1` for a d-regular graph on 2^d vertices — `2m=5` for d=3 and `3m=17` for d=4, neither soluble in integers. So 13 and 33 are impossible and U(Q_3)=14, U(Q_4)=34. Head re-scored its artefacts: `VERIFIED 14` / `VERIFIED 34`.
29. Its `divergence.md` is the strongest artefact the dry run produced: a per-idea provenance table separating what is literature (the |E|+1 bound, the equality condition), what is the blind runs' (DP, branch-and-bound, state merging) and what is new here (the down-degree count and the divisibility obstruction), plus the observation that the same argument gives `U(Q_d) >= d*2^{d-1}+2` for every d >= 3 — outside the cell, and beyond anything either blind run claims.
30. **The blind searches turned out to be unnecessary for this cell.** Two blind searchers spent ~2900 s of wall time and ~115k tokens proving computationally what one literature agent proved by hand in 397 s. This is the intended shape of 1L (blind first, literature after, never merged), and the dry run shows the comparison is worth its cost: without the blind runs there would be nothing to check the hand proof against, and the three routes agree on every value and on the full d=3 value distribution.
